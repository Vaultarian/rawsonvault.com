Jos's Timers

Ten timers. Pick one.

Puzzle
A piece drops in every tick. At zero the smiley face is finished.
Coke Bottle
It shakes harder and harder, and at zero the pressure wins.
Bomb
The burning fuse is the progress bar. Watch it get shorter.
Tsunami
At zero a wave sweeps in and carries the numbers away.
Rocket
Blasts off the top of the screen, then crashes back down at one.
Knock-the-Number
A ball flies in and knocks each digit off as it changes.
Running Man
A stopwatch counting up. Every footstep is one second.
Traffic Light
Red, yellow, green, round and round, once every minute.
Dying Sun
Starts dark. Gets brighter every second until it blows up.
Flipbook
A page turns every second, and the page you see is the time.
