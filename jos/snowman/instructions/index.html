❄️ It’s Hard to Build a Snowman – Game Rules

🎯 Goal

Build one complete snowman made of three snowballs stacked in size order:

3 → 2 → 1 (big at bottom, small at top)

When any tile has a stack 3-2-1, you win the level.

🧊 The Board

The world is a grid with:

  • Letters across the top (ABCDE.etc)
  • Numbers down the side (1234.etc)

Each tile is one of these types:

TileColourWhat it does
SnowWhiteRolling a ball across it can make the ball grow
GrassGreenNormal ground
WallBlackSolid. Cannot walk into or push balls into it

🙂 The Player

  • You are the little face icon 🙂 (or 😁 when you win)
  • Move using arrow keys or the on‑screen buttons
  • You cannot step on walls or leave the grid
