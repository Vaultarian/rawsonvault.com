Tutorial 3 — Corner Problems (Harder Challenge)

Welcome to Tutorial 3 — The Corner Problem (Harder Challenge)! This time, your goal is to build a snowman on **F1** — the far upper corner. Every snowball starts as size 1️⃣, but rolling over snow will make it grow: • 1️⃣ → 2️⃣ → 3️⃣ as you roll over snow. **Your setup:** • 1 snowball at C1 • 1 snowball at D2 • 1 snowball at E3 **Your task:** 1. Push the C1 snowball across to F1 to grow it into a 3️⃣. 2. Push the D2 snowball across to F2 to grow it into a 2️⃣. 3. Push the E3 snowball upward to E1, keeping it as a 1️⃣. 4. Stack them: move the 2️⃣ onto the 3️⃣ (F2 → F1), then the 1️⃣ onto the 2️⃣ (E1 → F1) to make your snowman ☃️. **Tip:** Column A and Row 4 are grass — no snow grows there. Everything else is snow, so plan your path carefully!
