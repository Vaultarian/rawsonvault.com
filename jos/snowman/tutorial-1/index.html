Tutorial 1 — How to Build a Snowman

Welcome to your first snowman lesson! The numbers you see on the snowballs show their sizes: • 1 is the smallest snowball. • 2 is the middle-sized snowball. • 3 is the largest snowball. To build a snowman, you must stack them in the correct order: 1️⃣ goes on top of 2️⃣, and 2️⃣ goes on top of 3️⃣. Step-by-step: 1. Push the medium snowball (2) so it rests on the large one (3). 2. Then push the small snowball (1) on top of the other two. When you have 1 on 2 on 3, you’ll see a ☃️ appear — your first snowman! Give it a try.
