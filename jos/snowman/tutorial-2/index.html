Tutorial 2 — Growing Snow

Welcome to Tutorial 2 — Learning about snow! When you push a snowball across snow, it grows: • 1️⃣ becomes 2️⃣ when it rolls over snow. • 2️⃣ becomes 3️⃣ when it rolls over snow again. • 3️⃣ stays 3️⃣ — it can’t get any bigger! Each time a snowball rolls, the snow beneath turns to grass. Grass cannot make a snowball grow, so plan your path carefully. To help you see: some grass has already been placed under the first snowball at C2 — it will not grow there. Only snow-covered tiles make a snowball bigger. Goal: Make the snowball at C4 grow to size 2 by rolling it carefully toward the big snowball at C6. Don’t let it become another 3! Remember: a snowball can only stack onto a bigger one — a 3 can’t go on another 3! Once you’ve grown the right sizes and stacked correctly, you’ll build your next snowman.
