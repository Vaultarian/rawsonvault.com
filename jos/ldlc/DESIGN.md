# LDLC — Level Devil Level Creator

Jos's idea, 30 Oct 2026. To build **after SEASONALITY! is finished** (about three weeks away).

## The big idea, in Jos's words

> "The reason I'm making it a level devil creator is so you can make your own levels. I know
> you can just play them, but I would also like to make my own."

So **making levels is the main thing.** PLAY is for playing the levels people make.

## What exists so far

- The start screen (`index.html`): LDLC logo, ▶ PLAY, 🛠 CREATE A LEVEL, a little devil the
  level keeps killing along the bottom, death counter. Both buttons say "Coming soon".
- **Not in any menu. Not published.**
