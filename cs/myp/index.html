MYP Design

Design cycle, digital design, and product design for Years 8 and 9 — this section is being built.

The MYP Design programme runs at St Leonards in Years 8 and 9. It is assessed against four criteria: Inquiring and Analysing (A), Developing Ideas (B), Creating the Solution (C), and Evaluating (D). Students work through the design cycle — investigating a problem, designing a solution, creating it, and evaluating its success against the original brief.

Digital design strands include web design, programming in Python, physical computing (micro:bit), and data literacy. Product design strands cover materials, prototyping, and the principles of human-centred design. Unit pages will be added as the Year 8 and Year 9 programmes are developed for St Leonards.
