MYP Sciences (Physics)

Integrated, criterion-referenced science for the Middle Years Programme — this section is being built.

The MYP sciences programme runs through Years 7–9 at St Leonards and is assessed against four criteria: Knowing and Understanding (A), Inquiring and Designing (B), Processing and Evaluating (C), and Reflecting on the Impacts of Science (D). Teaching is integrated across biology, chemistry, and physics, with framework units built around the conceptual and contextual underpinnings of the IB.

Unit pages for MYP Physics will be added as the Year 7–9 programme is developed for St Leonards.
