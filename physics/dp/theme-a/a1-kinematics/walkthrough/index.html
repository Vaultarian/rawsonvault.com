A.1 Kinematics — Visual Walkthrough

The whole of one-dimensional motion, one figure at a time.

Kinematics is the language you use to say what a body is doing before you ask why it does it. This page walks the whole of that language in order — motion diagrams, position, displacement, vectors, graphs, acceleration, and free fall — one figure at a time. Read it top to bottom the first time. After that, use the jump links to go straight to the thing you have forgotten.

This walkthrough covers one-dimensional motion. Projectile and two-dimensional motion — also part of A.1 — are not covered here; use the A.1 topic page for video support and worksheets on those.
