P1 Energy

Summary

Energy cannot be created or destroyed — only stored differently and transferred between stores. This topic builds the habit of naming the stores before and after a change, then putting numbers to them: kinetic, gravitational and elastic potential energy, thermal energy and specific heat capacity, and power as the rate of transfer.
