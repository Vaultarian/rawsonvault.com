Boiling 2.0 kg of Water

The water is already at 100 °C, so all of the energy goes into the change of state.

Calculate the energy needed to boil 2.0 kg of water that is already at 100 °C, turning 2.0 kg of liquid water into 2.0 kg of steam (gas).

What the energy does

Ek · Temperature and kinetic energy

  • Temperature stays at 100 °C while the water boils.
  • The particles keep the same speed, so kinetic energy stays the same.

Ep · Bonds and potential energy

  • The energy supplied breaks the remaining bonds, and the particles move far apart as steam, so potential energy increases.
  • Energy: E = m L (specific latent heat of vaporisation)
Show a full answer
  1. Liquid → vapour, so use the specific latent heat of vaporisation of water: 2 260 000 J/kg.
  2. E = m L = 2.0 × 2 260 000
  3. E = 4 520 000 J (4.52 MJ)

The temperature stays at 100 °C throughout. The energy breaks bonds between the particles, so their Ep increases and their Ek stays the same.
