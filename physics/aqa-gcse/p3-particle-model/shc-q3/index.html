Work it out, one step at a time

These steps use the question's own values. Write each line on your sheet as it appears: equation, substitution, answer with its unit.

What the energy does

Ek · The particles

  • The temperature rises.
  • The particles vibrate faster about their fixed positions, so their kinetic energy increases.
  • The pan stays solid with every bond intact, so potential energy stays the same.
  • Internal energy (total kinetic and potential energy of all the particles) goes up through Ek.

The equation

  • ΔE = m c Δθ is on the equation sheet.
  • m is the mass in kg.
  • c is the specific heat capacity in J/kg °C, from the table.
  • Δθ is the temperature change in °C: end temperature − start temperature = 70 − 20 = 50 °C.
Show a full answer

Δθ = 70 − 20 = 50 °C

ΔE = m c Δθ = 3.0 × 450 × 50 = 67 500 J

The energy raises the temperature of the iron, so the kinetic energy of its particles increases.
