Water to Steam

The same particles, the same mass, a far larger volume.

Calculate the volume of steam produced when 0.50 kg of water boils away completely. Density of steam = 0.60 kg/m³. Show the equation, the substitution and the answer with its unit.

Every particle of the water becomes a particle of the steam, so the mass is carried across the change of state. The particles spread out, so the volume changes. Move the sliders and count the particles on each side.

Carry the mass across

Stays the same

  • The number of particles: every water particle becomes a steam particle.
  • So the mass: m is the same number before and after.

Changes

  • The volume: the particles move far apart.
  • So the density: the same mass in a larger volume is a smaller density.

The working, one step at a time

The density equation is recalled from memory: it is not on the equation sheet.

  1. Carry the massMass is conserved, so the steam has m = 0.50 kg.
  2. Equationρ = m / V, rearranged for volume: V = m / ρ
  3. SubstitutionV = 0.50 / 0.60
  4. Answer with unitV = 0.83 m³ (0.833…)
  5. CompareAs water it filled only 0.00050 m³: the same particles now fill about 1700 times the volume.

Mass is conserved, so the steam has m = 0.50 kg. V = m / ρ = 0.50 / 0.60 = 0.83 m³ (0.833…). As water it filled only 0.00050 m³: the same particles now fill about 1700 times the volume.
