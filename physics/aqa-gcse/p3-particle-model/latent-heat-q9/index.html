3.5 Specific Latent Heat · Question 9

The Forgotten Ice Cubes

Five ice cubes left in a warm room: stage 1 melts the ice, stage 2 warms the water.

Higher · a two-stage problem

Someone puts 5 ice cubes in a glass, forgets about it, and leaves it for 12 hours. The ice absorbs energy from the room around it. First it melts, then the water warms up until it is in thermal equilibrium with the room: the same temperature, 20 °C.

Each ice cube has a mass of 0.10 kg and starts at 0 °C. How much energy has all of the ice absorbed, to turn it from solid ice at 0 °C into liquid water at 20 °C?

This page opens on the finished answer: all the ice melted and the water at 20 °C. Press Play to run the 12 hours again from the start, or drag the energy slider. The process has two stages, 1 and 2, and each stage has its own equation.

Two stages, two equations

1 · Melt the ice

  • A change of state: solid → liquid.
  • Temperature stays at 0 °C.
  • The energy breaks bonds between the particles, so their potential energy increases.
  • Energy: E = m L (specific latent heat of fusion)

2 · Warm the water

  • A temperature rise: 0 °C to 20 °C.
  • The particles move faster, so their kinetic energy increases.
  • The water stays liquid the whole time.
  • Energy: ΔE = m c Δθ (specific heat capacity of water)

Working, one step at a time

These steps use the question's own values. Try each line on paper, then reveal it.

  5. Stage 2 · equationE2 = m c Δθ

  8. Total · equationTotal energy = E1 + E2
  9. SubstituteTotal energy = 167 000 + 42 000
