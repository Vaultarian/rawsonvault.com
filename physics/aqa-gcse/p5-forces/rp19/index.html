RP19: Force, Mass and Acceleration

Trendline clips for the required practical data sheet

Add a trendline: Part A, Constant Mass, Changing Force

Draw the straight line of best fit through your acceleration and force points and show its equation.

Click the clip to watch it full screen.

  1. Click one of your data points. The Series options open.
  2. Scroll down and tick the Trend line box.
  3. Keep Type as Linear.
  4. Under Label, choose Use equation.
  5. Open Legend and set the legend font size so the equation is easy to read.
  6. Check: the line and its equation appear on your graph.

Add a trendline to a curve: Part B, Constant Force, Changing Mass

Fit a curve through your acceleration and total mass points and show its equation.

Click the clip to watch it full screen.

  1. Click one of your data points. The Series options open.
  2. Scroll down and tick the Trend line box.
  3. Open the Type menu and choose Power series. The line becomes a curve.
  4. Under Label, choose Use equation.
  5. Open Legend and set the legend font size so the equation is easy to read.
  6. Check: the curve and its equation appear on your graph.
