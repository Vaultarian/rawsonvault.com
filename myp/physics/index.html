MYP Physics

Criterion-referenced physics for Years 8 and 9, taught through inquiry and built around a statement of inquiry for each unit

MYP Physics runs across Years 8 and 9 as part of the IB Middle Years Programme. It is not exam-driven in the way GCSE is. Instead, each unit is framed by a key concept, a set of related concepts, a global context, and a statement of inquiry that ties the physics to a bigger idea. Students build subject knowledge while developing the skills of a working scientist: designing investigations, processing and evaluating data, and reflecting on what science means for people and the planet.

Assessment is criterion-referenced. Rather than a single percentage, work is judged against four strands, each marked out of 8 and reported separately, so a student can see exactly where they are strong and where to push next.

The four MYP Sciences criteria

  • A — Knowing and understanding. Recalling and applying scientific knowledge to explain phenomena and solve problems, including in unfamiliar situations.
  • B — Inquiring and designing. Asking a focused question, forming a hypothesis, and planning a fair, safe investigation with controlled variables.
  • C — Processing and evaluating. Presenting and interpreting data, drawing valid conclusions, and evaluating the method and its limitations.
  • D — Reflecting on the impacts of science. Discussing how science is applied to real problems, weighing its implications, and using scientific language and sources correctly.
