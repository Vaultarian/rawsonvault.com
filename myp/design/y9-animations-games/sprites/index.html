Game Lab Sprite Gallery

Every sprite in the Game Lab library on one page: 2,553 sprites in 22 categories. 47 of them are animated, which means they move on their own; they carry a moves label and play here. Every other sprite is a still picture that moves when your code moves it.

To use a sprite in your project
  1. Find it here and click it. Its name is copied for you.
  2. In Game Lab, open the Animation tab and choose a new animation from the library.
  3. Paste the name into the library's search box and pick the sprite.

No sprite matches that search. Try a shorter word, or choose All.
