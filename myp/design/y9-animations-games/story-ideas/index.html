Animated Scene: 20 Story Ideas

Your mini-project is an animated scene that tells a small story. Follow the three steps, then borrow one of the 20 ideas below or invent your own. Press Run on any idea to watch it play.

  1. Choose a backgroundPick one from the library. The background decides where your story happens. See all the backgrounds.
  2. Think of a storyWho is in this place? It could be an animal, a person or a machine. What are they doing? One sentence is enough.
  3. Choose the movementUse the counter pattern for something that travels. Use random numbers for something that shakes.

The two kinds of movement

PatternCode inside draw()What you seeGood for
Counter patternsprite.x = sprite.x + 2;The sprite travels smoothly to the right and off the screen. It happens once each time you press Run.Walking, driving, flying, swimming
sprite.x = sprite.x - 2;The same, travelling left.A sprite that faces left
sprite.y = sprite.y - 2;The sprite rises. Use + to make it sink.Rockets, balloons, bubbles, landings
sprite.rotation = sprite.rotation + 5;The sprite spins on the spot.Wheels, balls, suns
Random numberssprite.rotation = randomNumber(-5, 5);The sprite shakes from side to side.Waving, shivering, roaring, laughing
sprite.x = randomNumber(198, 202);The sprite jitters around one spot. Add the same for y to make it hover.Bees, butterflies, anything nervous

A worked example: Lift-off

var sky = createSprite(200, 200);
sky.setAnimation("space");

var rocket = createSprite(210, 300);
rocket.setAnimation("sticker_32");
rocket.scale = 0.3;

var astronaut = createSprite(325, 310);
astronaut.setAnimation("astronaut");
astronaut.scale = 0.2;

function draw() {
  rocket.y = rocket.y - 3;
  astronaut.rotation = randomNumber(-8, 8);
  drawSprites();
  fill("white");
  textSize(24);
  text("3, 2, 1... lift-off!", 100, 40);
}
  • The background is a sprite too. It is created first, so it sits behind everything else.
  • Add each picture to your project in the Animation tab before you use its name in setAnimation.
  • Library sprites are large. A scale between 0.1 and 0.4 fits most of them into the scene.
  • The text comes after drawSprites(), so it is drawn on top.

20 story ideas

Each idea names its background and its sprites. Click a name to copy it, then paste it into the library search in Game Lab. The whole library is in the sprite gallery.

Invent your own

Finish these three sentences and you have a plan. My scene is in (a background). The thing that travels is (a sprite), and it goes (left, right, up or down). The thing that shakes is (a sprite), because (a reason from your story).
