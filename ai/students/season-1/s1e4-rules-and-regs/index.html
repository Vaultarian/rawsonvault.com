S1E4 Rules and Regs

Writing the rules we want to live by when we use AI together

What this lesson is about

Every club needs a few shared rules, and one that uses AI needs them more than most. In this lesson you will look at how the club uses AI tools safely and fairly, and then help write the ideas that go into our own Class Constitution. The guiding aim is simple, AI should be a thinking partner, never a shortcut that does your thinking for you.

What you'll be able to do

  • I can explain why a group that uses AI together needs shared rules, and say what those rules protect.
  • I can put the four commitments in the club pledge into my own words.
  • I can bring a proposed rule to the group and argue for it when we write the Class Constitution.

A little background

The club's approach rests on a short pledge that every member makes, to keep yourself and others safe by never sharing personal or sensitive information with AI, to use AI to support your thinking rather than replace it, to be honest about when and how you used it, and to speak up if AI ever shows something harmful, biased, or worrying. Working with the Gem, you will gather your own ideas to bring to the group when the class writes its constitution together.

Key words

Class Constitution
The set of rules this club writes for itself about how AI gets used here.
Personal data
Anything that identifies a real person — a name, a school, an address, a photograph.
Attribution
Saying openly when and how you used AI on a piece of work.
