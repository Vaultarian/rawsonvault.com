S2E1 Prompt Engineering 101

The way you ask is the answer you get

What this lesson is about

AI systems do not understand your questions the way a person does. They respond to the instructions in your prompt, so how you write a prompt changes how useful the answer is. Designing prompts on purpose, instead of typing the first thing that comes to mind, is called prompt engineering. In this lesson you experiment with different prompt styles and watch the quality of the response change in front of you.

What you'll be able to do

  • I can design clear prompts that guide AI systems toward more useful, accurate, and relevant responses.
  • I can recognise how different prompt structures influence the quality, depth, and reliability of AI output.

Small changes make a big difference. Prompts that give the AI a role, name the task clearly, add some context, and ask for a particular format tend to produce stronger answers than a short, vague question. Today's Gem walks you up five levels, adding one technique at a time, so you can see exactly what each one does to the result.

Key words

Prompt
Everything you send the model — the question and every instruction wrapped around it.
Prompt engineering
Designing that wrapping on purpose, instead of typing the first thing that comes to mind.
Context
The background you hand over so the answer fits your situation rather than a generic one.
Output format
Telling the model the shape you want back: a table, five bullets, a paragraph, a checklist.
