S2E5 Brainstorming

The constraint is where creativity begins

What this lesson is about

Most people think brainstorming means throwing out as many ideas as possible with no limits. Designers and engineers know the opposite often works better: a hard constraint, a weight limit or a tight budget, is where the real creativity starts. Today you pick a real design challenge and work it through with AI as your partner, following the design arc, state a vivid constraint, hit a wall when the obvious idea fails, reframe the problem, and discover a better solution. AI checks the numbers and surfaces what you missed. You decide what matters.

What you'll be able to do

  • I can use the design arc — constraint, wall, reframe, upgrade — to brainstorm with AI, and I can explain why the quality of human thinking, not just human prompting, determines the quality of the outcome.

Two of the club's commandments matter a lot today. AI is trying to please you, so left unchallenged it tends to validate your design rather than stress-test it, and you have to actively ask it to find the flaws. AI can also give accurate but dangerously outdated information, citing a real source that is decades old. Watch for both.

Key words

Constraint
A hard limit — a weight, a budget, a deadline — that the design has to live inside.
Reframe
Changing the question when the obvious answer hits a wall, rather than pushing harder at it.
Sycophancy
An AI's pull toward agreeing with you. Left unchallenged it validates a design instead of testing it.
